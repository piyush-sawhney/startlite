# 50 Micro-Businesses: Customers and Daily Pain

> Part of the StartLite docs. Start at [README](README.md).

These 50 shops are the reference users. Each row names who the customer is, what
goes wrong today, and the one behavior that fixes it. Items and their flows live
in [business-catalog.md](business-catalog.md). This page carries no item list and
no price.

The recurring pains:

- *"Bhaiya, rate card bhejo"*: a photo of a crumpled paper menu is the catalog.
- *"Aapka kitna baaki tha?"*: credit lives in a diary and gets disputed.
- *"Chhutta nahi hai"*: cash and UPI are reconciled by hand at night.
- *"Order kya tha?"*: orders disappear inside a WhatsApp group.
