# 50 Indian Micro-Businesses: Items, Customers & Ledger Realities

> Part of the StartLite documentation. See [README.md](README.md) and [category-packs.md](category-packs.md).

StartLite is built specifically for **micro-business owners** across India who currently manage their businesses using **pen-and-paper diaries (bahi-khata), Excel spreadsheets, or chaotic WhatsApp message threads**. 

It is **not** a bloated ERP, nor an advertising marketplace, nor a paid WhatsApp Business API chatbot. It is a quiet, ultra-fast digital catalog and cash ledger designed to eliminate the daily headaches of:
- *"Bhaiya, rate card / menu bhejo"* (sending photos of crumpled paper menus)
- *"Aapka kitna baaki tha?"* (lost credit / Udhar disputes)
- *"Chhutta nahi hai / cash nahi hai"* (manual UPI reconciliation)
- *"Order kya tha, bhool gaye"* (lost orders in WhatsApp group chats)

## Usability Guidelines Derived from These 50 Businesses

1. **Zero Reading Required**: A carpenter with sawdust on his hands or a chaat vendor with chutney on his apron cannot read long instruction manuals. Actions must be 1-tap, visual, and unmistakable.
2. **Never Show Public Bank Details**: A shop's public storefront is browsed by strangers on the internet. Bank account numbers and IFSC codes must never be plastered on the web. They belong strictly in private WhatsApp messages sent when payment is due.
3. **The Store Belongs to the Merchant**: The shop name and products must be the hero. StartLite is proud infrastructure, sitting gracefully in the footer.
4. **Color Psychology That Matches Human Nature**:
   - **Emerald Green**: Money received in cash/UPI, order accepted, WhatsApp action.
   - **Amber / Orange**: Money owed by a customer (Udhar), order waiting for review.
   - **Crimson / Red**: Money paid out for supplies/rent/veggies, order declined.
5. **No WhatsApp Business API Charges**: Micro-owners make ₹200–₹1,000 margins per day. Charging them per message or forcing them into meta business verification kills their livelihoods. StartLite opens native WhatsApp with pre-formatted messages for 100% free peer-to-peer delivery.
